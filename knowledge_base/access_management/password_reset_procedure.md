Title: Password Reset Procedure
Department: Access Management
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Describe the self-service and support-assisted process for resetting a forgotten TCS Enterprise account password.
Applicable Issues: Forgot password, password reset, need to reset my password, can't remember login password, password expired.

Symptoms:
- Employee cannot log in because they have forgotten their password
- Employee's password has expired per the corporate password policy
- Employee wants to proactively change their password

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Direct the employee to the Employee Portal login page and select "Forgot Password."
2. Employee verifies their identity using the registered recovery email or MFA-registered device.
3. Employee sets a new password meeting the corporate complexity requirements (minimum 12 characters, mix of upper/lower case, number, and symbol).
4. If self-service reset is unavailable (e.g., recovery method not registered), the employee must contact Access Management with proof of identity for a manual reset.
5. For manual resets, Access Management verifies identity through the employee's manager or HR record before issuing a temporary password.
6. Employee is required to change the temporary password on next login.

Expected Result: Employee regains access to their account with a new password meeting policy requirements.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Access Management for manual identity verification if self-service reset fails or the recovery method is unavailable. Escalate to Administration if there is suspicion the reset request is fraudulent.

Important Notes: Support agents must never ask an employee for their current or new password. Password resets always occur through the secure portal flow or a verified manual process. This document does not cover account lockouts due to failed login attempts (see Account Lockout Troubleshooting).
