Title: Asset Request Procedure
Department: Asset Management
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Describe how employees request new hardware assets such as monitors, docking stations, headsets, or peripherals.
Applicable Issues: Need a new monitor, requesting a docking station, need a headset, requesting additional hardware, need a second laptop charger.

Symptoms:
- Employee requires additional hardware to perform their job (e.g., external monitor for remote work)
- Employee's existing peripheral is missing or was never issued
- Team is requesting standard equipment for a new project

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Employee submits an Asset Request through the Employee Portal under Asset Management, specifying the item and business justification.
2. Standard items (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset, docking station) are auto-approved if within the employee's role-based entitlement.
3. Non-standard or high-cost items require manager approval before fulfillment.
4. Asset Management checks inventory availability and, if in stock, ships or arranges pickup of the item.
5. If out of stock, the employee is notified of the estimated restock date or offered an alternative model.
6. The asset is logged against the employee's asset record for tracking.

Expected Result: Employee receives the requested hardware within the standard SLA (typically 3-7 business days depending on stock).

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Asset Management if an approved request is not fulfilled within SLA, or if the requested item requires budget approval beyond standard entitlement.

Important Notes: This document covers new/additional hardware requests only. Replacement of failed laptops is covered in Laptop Replacement Procedure.
