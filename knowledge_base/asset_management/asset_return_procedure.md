Title: Asset Return Procedure
Department: Asset Management
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Describe the process for returning company-issued assets, such as during offboarding or equipment upgrades.
Applicable Issues: Returning laptop after resignation, offboarding equipment return, returning old monitor after upgrade, unused equipment pickup.

Symptoms:
- Employee is leaving the company and needs to return issued equipment
- Employee received a replacement device and needs to return the old one
- Department has unused equipment to return to inventory

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Asset Management generates a return checklist based on the employee's current asset record (laptop, monitor, peripherals, access badge).
2. Employee is provided instructions for packaging and shipping or dropping off equipment at the designated location.
3. All company data on returned laptops is backed up (if required) and then the device is wiped per data retention policy.
4. Asset Management confirms receipt and updates the asset record to reflect the item as returned.
5. For offboarding, confirm all assets are returned before final access revocation is completed by Access Management.

Expected Result: All company assets are returned, wiped, and logged as returned in the inventory system.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Asset Management leadership if equipment is not returned within the standard offboarding window, and to Administration if there is concern about missing or unreturned company property.

Important Notes: Data wiping must follow the standard retention policy; do not skip backup steps for departing employees without manager confirmation that no data needs to be preserved.
