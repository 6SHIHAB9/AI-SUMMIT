Title: Payslip Access FAQ
Department: Payroll Support
Document Type: FAQ
Purpose: Answer common questions about viewing, downloading, and understanding payslips.
Applicable Issues: Where to find payslip, payslip not available yet, download payslip as PDF, payslip missing for a specific month, understanding payslip deductions.

Symptoms:
- Employee cannot find where to view their payslip
- Payslip for the current or a past month is not showing
- Employee wants to download a payslip as a PDF for personal records
- Employee has questions about a deduction line item on their payslip

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Direct the employee to Payroll Portal > Payslips, where payslips are listed by month.
2. Payslips are typically generated and available within 2 business days after the monthly pay run.
3. If a payslip for a past month is missing, confirm the employee's start date; payslips are only generated from the employee's first full pay cycle.
4. To download, the employee selects the relevant month and clicks "Download PDF."
5. For questions about specific deduction line items (tax, benefits, other withholdings), forward to Payroll Support for a detailed breakdown, as agents should not interpret deduction amounts without payroll data access.

Expected Result: Employee can locate, view, and download their payslips, with deduction questions routed appropriately.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Payroll Support if a payslip is missing beyond the expected generation window, or if the employee disputes a deduction amount.

Important Notes: This document covers payslip access only. Portal login issues are covered in Payroll Portal Troubleshooting.
