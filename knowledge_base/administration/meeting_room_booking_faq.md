Title: Meeting Room Booking FAQ
Department: Administration
Document Type: FAQ
Purpose: Answer common questions about booking and managing meeting rooms at TCS Enterprise offices.
Applicable Issues: How to book a meeting room, meeting room booking conflict, can't find meeting room in booking system, need to cancel a meeting room booking, meeting room equipment not working.

Symptoms:
- Employee does not know how to book a meeting room
- Employee sees a booking conflict when trying to reserve a room
- A booked meeting room does not appear correctly in the calendar
- Employee needs to cancel or change an existing room booking

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Meeting rooms are booked through the Employee Portal calendar integration by selecting "Add Room" when creating a calendar event.
2. If a booking conflict appears, the employee can view room availability directly in the booking tool to select an open time slot or alternative room.
3. To cancel a booking, the employee removes the room from the calendar event, which automatically releases the reservation.
4. If a room does not appear as booked despite confirmation, ask the employee to refresh the Employee Portal calendar sync or check for a delayed calendar update.
5. For equipment issues within a booked room (e.g., broken projector), submit a general Administration request for facilities to inspect the equipment.

Expected Result: Employee can successfully book, view, and cancel meeting room reservations without conflicts.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Administration if room booking conflicts persist due to a system sync issue, or if equipment issues are reported repeatedly for the same room.

Important Notes: This document covers meeting room booking logistics only, not IT equipment troubleshooting for laptops or software used during meetings.
