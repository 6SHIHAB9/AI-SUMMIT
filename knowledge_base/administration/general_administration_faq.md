Title: General Administration FAQ
Department: Administration
Document Type: FAQ
Purpose: Answer common general administrative questions that do not fall under IT, HR, or Payroll.
Applicable Issues: Requesting a visitor badge, office access badge not working, parking access questions, general facilities questions, mail and courier handling.

Symptoms:
- Employee's office access badge is not working at the entrance
- Employee needs to request a visitor badge for a guest
- Employee has a question about parking access or facilities
- Employee needs to send or receive mail/courier through the office

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. For a non-working access badge, confirm the badge is registered and active by checking the Administration facilities system; badges deactivate automatically after extended leave and must be reactivated on return.
2. For visitor badges, the employee submits a Visitor Request through the Employee Portal at least 24 hours in advance, including visitor name and purpose of visit.
3. For parking access questions, direct the employee to the Facilities section of the Employee Portal where parking policies and requests are listed.
4. For mail and courier handling, incoming mail is logged at the reception desk and employees are notified by email when a package arrives.
5. For any facilities issue not covered above (e.g., meeting room equipment, office supplies), the employee should submit a general Administration request through the Employee Portal.

Expected Result: Employee's general administrative request or issue is resolved or routed to the correct facilities process.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Administration directly for physical security concerns (badge access to secure areas) or facilities issues requiring in-person attention.

Important Notes: This document covers general facilities and administrative topics only. It does not cover IT, HR, or Payroll matters, which are handled by their respective departments.
