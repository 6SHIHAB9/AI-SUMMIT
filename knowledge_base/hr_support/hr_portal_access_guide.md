Title: HR Portal Access Guide
Department: HR Support
Document Type: Guide
Purpose: Help employees access and navigate the TCS Enterprise HR Portal for personal and employment information.
Applicable Issues: Can't log into HR Portal, HR Portal showing an error, don't know how to find HR documents, HR Portal access request.

Symptoms:
- Employee cannot log into the HR Portal despite having Employee Portal access
- HR Portal shows a permissions error when accessing certain sections
- Employee is unsure how to locate specific HR documents (contract, policies, benefits information)

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Confirm the employee is using their standard company credentials to log into the HR Portal (same login as the Employee Portal).
2. If login fails, confirm whether this is a password issue (see Password Reset Procedure) or account lockout (see Account Lockout Troubleshooting).
3. If logged in but certain sections show a permissions error, confirm the employee's HR Portal role matches their current employment status and department.
4. Guide the employee to the relevant HR Portal section: Documents, Benefits, Leave Management, or Personal Information.
5. If a specific document is missing (e.g., contract not visible), forward the request to HR Support to confirm the document has been uploaded to the employee's record.

Expected Result: Employee can log into the HR Portal and access the relevant sections and documents.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to HR Support if a document is confirmed missing from the employee's record, or if permissions appear misconfigured for the employee's role.

Important Notes: This document covers HR Portal access and navigation only. Leave requests are covered in Leave Management FAQ, and payroll-specific access is covered in Payroll Portal Troubleshooting.
