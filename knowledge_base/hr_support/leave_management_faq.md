Title: Leave Management FAQ
Department: HR Support
Document Type: FAQ
Purpose: Answer common questions about requesting, tracking, and managing leave through the HR Portal.
Applicable Issues: How to apply for leave, leave balance incorrect, leave request stuck pending, leave not showing in HR Portal, cancel a leave request.

Symptoms:
- Employee does not know how to submit a leave request
- Leave balance displayed in the HR Portal appears incorrect
- Submitted leave request has been pending approval for an extended period
- Employee needs to cancel or modify an already-submitted leave request

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. To apply for leave, the employee navigates to HR Portal > Leave Management > New Request, selects leave type and dates, and submits for manager approval.
2. Leave balance is calculated based on accrual rules for the employee's leave type and tenure; discrepancies should first be checked against the accrual schedule shown in the portal.
3. If a leave request has been pending for more than 3 business days, HR Support can send a reminder to the approving manager.
4. To cancel a submitted request, the employee selects the request in Leave Management and chooses "Withdraw" (only available before the leave start date).
5. For leave balance discrepancies that cannot be explained by the accrual schedule, HR Support reviews the employee's leave history manually.

Expected Result: Employee can successfully submit, track, and manage leave requests, with an accurate leave balance.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to HR Support for manual leave balance correction if a genuine discrepancy is confirmed, or if a manager is unresponsive to a pending leave request beyond 3 business days.

Important Notes: This document covers standard leave requests. Special leave types (e.g., extended medical leave) may follow a separate policy process managed directly by HR Support.
