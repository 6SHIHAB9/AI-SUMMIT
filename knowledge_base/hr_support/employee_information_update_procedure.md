Title: Employee Information Update Procedure
Department: HR Support
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Describe how employees can update personal information such as address, emergency contact, or bank details on file.
Applicable Issues: Update home address, change emergency contact, update phone number, change bank account for payroll, update personal details in HR Portal.

Symptoms:
- Employee needs to update their home address or contact details
- Employee needs to update emergency contact information
- Employee needs to change bank account details for payroll deposit

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Employee navigates to HR Portal > Personal Information and edits the relevant field directly for basic details (address, phone, emergency contact).
2. Basic detail changes are saved immediately and do not require approval.
3. For sensitive changes such as bank account details for payroll, the employee must submit the update through HR Portal > Personal Information > Payment Details, which requires identity re-verification.
4. Bank detail changes are held for 24 hours before taking effect, and a confirmation notification is sent to the employee's registered email to detect potential fraud.
5. If the employee did not initiate a bank detail change notification they received, they must contact HR Support and Administration immediately.

Expected Result: Employee's information is updated accurately and securely, with sensitive changes protected by verification and notification.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate immediately to Administration if an employee reports an unrecognized change to their bank or payment details, as this may indicate account compromise.

Important Notes: HR Support will never ask an employee for their bank account details over chat, email, or phone; all changes must go through the verified HR Portal flow.
