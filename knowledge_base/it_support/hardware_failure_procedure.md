Title: Hardware Failure Procedure
Department: IT Support
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Define the steps for diagnosing and handling suspected physical hardware failures on company-issued devices.
Applicable Issues: Laptop won't power on, screen is black or shows no display, keyboard keys not responding, battery not charging, laptop shuts down unexpectedly, charging port not working.

Symptoms:
- Laptop does not power on even when plugged in
- Screen remains black despite the power light being on
- Battery percentage does not increase while charging
- Laptop randomly powers off during use
- Physical damage such as a cracked screen or broken hinge

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Confirm the charger and power outlet are functioning by testing with a different charger or outlet if available.
2. Perform a hard reset by holding the power button for 15-20 seconds, then attempt to power on.
3. Check for any visible physical damage (cracked screen, liquid exposure, damaged charging port).
4. If the laptop powers on but the display remains black, attempt connecting an external monitor to isolate whether the issue is the internal display or the motherboard.
5. Document all findings, including error lights, beep codes, or visible damage.
6. If no resolution is found through basic diagnostics, the device must be submitted for physical hardware inspection.

Expected Result: Either the device is restored to working condition, or a confirmed hardware fault is documented for repair or replacement.

Escalation Criteria: Any confirmed physical damage, failure to power on after a hard reset, or suspected motherboard/battery failure must be escalated to the IT Support hardware team for physical inspection and possible Laptop Replacement Procedure.

Important Notes: Do not attempt to open the laptop chassis or replace internal components without hardware team authorization. This document does not cover general slowness (see Laptop Performance Troubleshooting).
