Title: Employee Onboarding IT Setup
Department: IT Support
Document Type: SOP
Purpose: Outline the standard IT setup steps for new employees joining TCS Enterprise.
Applicable Issues: New hire laptop not set up, new employee has no accounts, first day IT access issues, onboarding checklist questions.

Symptoms:
- New employee does not have a laptop on their start date
- New employee cannot log into the Employee Portal
- New employee has no email account or Teams access
- Manager asking about onboarding IT checklist status

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Confirm the new hire's start date and department were submitted by HR Support at least 5 business days in advance.
2. IT Support provisions a company laptop pre-imaged with the standard software bundle (see Software Installation Guide).
3. Access Management creates the employee's core accounts: Employee Portal, email, and Teams, following the Access Request Procedure.
4. HR Support ensures the employee is added to the correct department distribution lists and org chart.
5. On the first day, IT Support verifies the employee can log in, connect to Corporate Wi-Fi, and access the VPN if remote work is required.
6. Any missing accounts or access issues are logged and resolved same-day where possible.

Expected Result: New employee has a working laptop and all core accounts active on their first day.

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Access Management if core accounts are not provisioned within 24 hours of the requested start date. Escalate to HR Support if the new hire record is missing or incomplete.

Important Notes: This document covers standard onboarding only. Requests for elevated or non-standard software/access must go through the relevant Software Support or Access Management approval process.
