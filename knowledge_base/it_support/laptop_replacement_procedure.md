Title: Laptop Replacement Procedure
Department: IT Support
Document Type: Procedure
Purpose: Describe the process for requesting and receiving a replacement laptop due to confirmed hardware failure, loss, or theft.
Applicable Issues: Laptop replacement request, lost laptop, stolen laptop, irreparable hardware damage, end-of-life device refresh.

Symptoms:
- Confirmed unrepairable hardware failure from the Hardware Failure Procedure
- Laptop was lost or stolen
- Laptop is beyond the standard 3-4 year refresh cycle and eligible for upgrade

Troubleshooting / Procedure:
1. Confirm the reason for replacement (hardware failure, loss, theft, or scheduled refresh) and gather supporting documentation such as the hardware inspection report.
2. If the laptop was lost or stolen, the employee must report this to IT Support and Administration immediately so the device can be remotely locked and access revoked.
3. Submit a replacement request through the Employee Portal under Asset Management > Request New Asset.
4. IT Support verifies asset eligibility and manager approval where required.
5. Once approved, a replacement laptop is imaged with standard software and prepared for handover.
6. The employee's data is migrated or restored from backup where available.
7. The old device (if recovered) is collected for wiping and disposal per asset retirement procedures.

Expected Result: Employee receives a fully configured replacement laptop within the standard SLA (typically 3-5 business days, subject to stock availability).

Escalation Criteria: Escalate to Asset Management if no replacement stock is available, or to Administration if the loss/theft involves potential data exposure requiring a security incident report.

Important Notes: Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately regardless of time of day, as delayed reporting increases risk of unauthorized data access.
